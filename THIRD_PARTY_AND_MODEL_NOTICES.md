## Downloaded models

This repository and its source-only launch do not distribute ASR, voice activity detection, Sortformer, Pyannote, or other model weights.

When you choose to download a model, FluidAudio/Hugging Face provides the model from its upstream source. Model terms, use restrictions, and attribution requirements are separate from Antrum's Apache-2.0 source license. Review the upstream model documentation and license before download or redistribution.
